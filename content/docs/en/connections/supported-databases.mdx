## Connection methods

PostgreSQL-, MySQL-, SQL Server-, MongoDB- and Redis-compatible engines accept their protocol’s connection URLs. Snowflake, BigQuery, Cassandra, ScyllaDB and Keyspaces use dedicated forms. SQLite and DuckDB use file paths. ClickHouse, Elasticsearch and OpenSearch also use their connection forms.
