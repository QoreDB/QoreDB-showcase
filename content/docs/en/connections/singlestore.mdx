# SingleStore

The **SingleStore** driver is included in Core since v0.1.39. It uses the MySQL protocol with its own connection identity and capability limits.

## Connect

1. Open **New Connection** and select **SingleStore**.
2. Enter the host, port (**3306** by default), credentials and database or namespace.
3. Configure TLS for your endpoint, choose the environment and use **Test Connection** before saving.

Cloud hosts under `*.svc.singlestore.com` are detected automatically. Otherwise choose SingleStore explicitly. Enter the database and credentials for your endpoint.

```text
mysql://USER:PASSWORD@HOST:3306/DATABASE?ssl-mode=required
```

Replace the uppercase placeholders with your connection details. For compatible engines, verify the selected driver after importing a URL; the protocol alone may identify only the parent engine.

## Capabilities and limits

Uses the MySQL protocol with engine-specific session handling. The visual DDL editor hides foreign keys, CHECK constraints and uniqueness controls that do not match this driver’s capabilities.

## Troubleshooting

If the connection fails, check the endpoint, port, credentials and certificate settings. If a control is unavailable after connecting, check the driver’s capabilities before changing permissions.
